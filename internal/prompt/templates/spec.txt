## Verdict
Two sentences. Is this design working, and what is the single highest-leverage fix?

## Keep
Two or three things that already work and must survive a redesign — a pattern, a piece
of hierarchy, a brand move. Name each and say why it earns its place. A critique that
only lists problems invites throwing out what was right.

## Findings
A table with columns: Screen, Issue, Severity (high/medium/low), Fix. One row per
issue, ordered by severity. Every row must cite something visible in a screenshot — no
generic advice that could apply to any app.

Across the whole table, cover all four of these lenses; do not skip one because it is
harder to see:
- Usability — can the user reach their goal; are actions obvious; any wasted steps.
- Visual hierarchy — reading order, emphasis, whitespace, type carrying the structure.
- Consistency — spacing, colour, and type against the brand constraints and against
  each other; do similar elements behave alike.
- Accessibility — with concrete checks, not a mention: text contrast against its
  background (name the pair and whether it clears WCAG AA 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large
  text), touch/click target size, and text legibility at real sizes.

## Tokens
The token set you would standardise on: colors with hex and role, type scale with
sizes and weights, spacing scale, radii, elevation. Concrete values only.

## Sequence
The order to apply the fixes in, and why that order.

No preamble, no closing summary, no offers to help further.
